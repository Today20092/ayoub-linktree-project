# AI-agent discoverability for Ayoub Abedrabbo

Research date: November 6, 2026. Research only; no website, account, analytics, or deployment changes.

## Recommendation

Start with the existing website, free search-provider reports, a small manual buyer-prompt panel, and existing Cloudflare analytics. Do not buy a visibility subscription or install a new analytics stack before establishing a baseline. The site already publishes unusually clear service facts; the missing evidence is whether real products retrieve, cite, and accurately recommend them.

Five credible open-source tools are compared below, plus Cloudflare's free proprietary crawl dashboard. None provides unlimited, zero-cost measurement of all consumer AI products. Open-source evaluation software can be free while the models and web-search APIs it calls are paid. A local model supplied with Ayoub's page measures understanding of that supplied content, not spontaneous discovery.

## Measure five different things

| Layer                                    | Useful evidence                                                                                                                   | What it cannot establish                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Crawl eligibility and indexing           | HTTP status, robots/meta controls, canonical, sitemap, provider URL inspection                                                    | That an assistant recommends Ayoub                                                                  |
| Brand mentions and citations in products | Saved answers from actual ChatGPT Search, Google AI experiences, Copilot, Claude search, or Perplexity; provider citation reports | Universal ranking, market share, or a booking                                                       |
| Crawler activity                         | Edge/request logs or crawl analytics, with bot identity confidence and response status                                            | That the fetch produced a displayed answer or user click                                            |
| Referrals and leads                      | Referrer/landing-page reports, received inquiries, source self-report, confirmed bookings                                         | Every citation or assistant impression; many sessions lose attribution                              |
| API approximation                        | Repeated prompts against a documented model and search configuration                                                              | The answer shown by the consumer app with its own orchestration, location, history, and experiments |

Treat an uncited brand mention separately from an owned-domain citation, and an owned-domain citation separately from an actual recommendation. A prompt containing Ayoub's name is an accuracy test; a prompt without it is a discovery test. These are proposed measurement definitions, not provider-standard metrics.

## Provider requirements and controls

### Google Search and local identity

Google's current optimization guide says ordinary SEO remains relevant: pages must be indexed, snippet-eligible, and included in Search generative AI features. Clear, useful content matters; no special AI schema is required. Google explicitly says it ignores `llms.txt` for Search visibility. These are eligibility conditions, not inclusion promises. [Google optimization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

An important date-sensitive change: Google's help pages state that its Search generative AI inclusion control and separate generative AI performance insights rolled out worldwide on August 31, 2026. The control defaults to inclusion; verify the actual property setting later rather than assuming it. The performance report describes impressions by page, country, and device; it may be absent when impressions are insufficient. Its absence does not establish zero visibility. [Inclusion-control help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024), [performance-report help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139).

There is a documentation inconsistency: the older AI-features page still describes AI traffic within the overall Web performance report. Use the specific new report help for the current measurement plan, retain overall Search trends, and verify what the actual property exposes. Neither page was treated as evidence about Ayoub's account. [Older AI-features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

Keep the existing Alpha Bravo Media/Ayoub identity and service facts consistent across the site and the linked business listing. Confirm ownership, category, service area, contact details, and website destination through the owner later; this research did not inspect or edit the listing. Genuine client evidence and accurate project case studies are better candidates than bulk AI-written location pages or manufactured mentions; this is an editorial recommendation, not a ranking promise.

### Bing, Copilot, and IndexNow

Bing's AI Performance public-preview announcement, dated February 10, 2026, documents citation reporting across Microsoft Copilot, Bing AI summaries, and selected partner integrations, including cited URLs and changes over time. It is a useful first-party source of actual citation evidence, with a narrower scope than “all AI assistants.” Do not infer a recommendation position or ChatGPT-wide visibility from it. [Microsoft announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/).

Later, inspect the service URL and sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools. Consider IndexNow only for meaningful changes to public pages: its protocol uses a hosted ownership key and URL notifications. It is a discovery/freshness signal to participating engines, not a citation measurement service. Acceptance does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or appearance; do not repeatedly submit unchanged pages or expose token-bearing gallery links. No submission or key creation was performed. [Protocol documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation), [IndexNow FAQ](https://www.indexnow.org/faq).

### Cloudflare and structured data

Cloudflare AI Crawl Control is available on all plans, but overlapping WAF and bot rules can still block desired crawlers. Before changing anything, the owner should inspect successful and unsuccessful requests to the hire page and discovery endpoints. Preserve protection on administration, uploads, and private galleries. An allow setting in one screen does not prove access through every layer. [Overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/), [WAF interaction](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/configuration/ai-crawl-control-with-waf/), [bot interaction](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/configuration/ai-crawl-control-with-bots/).

Keep the existing schema truthful and aligned with visible text. `Service` supports provider, service area, and offers; schema describes the offer but does not compel citation. Schema validation and Google's supported rich-result eligibility are different checks. Do not invent a public street address, reviews, ratings, or availability to satisfy a validator. The current service-area business may not qualify for every LocalBusiness rich-result requirement. [Schema.org Service](https://schema.org/Service), [Google LocalBusiness requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business).

`llms.txt` remains an author-published proposal, currently labeled v2, not a universal retrieval contract. Publishing labs' documentation indexes is evidence of use for documentation navigation, not proof that their consumer search products use Ayoub's file or boost it. Maintain the existing concise summaries when the offer changes; do not prioritize another format migration or `llms-full.txt` generator without a demonstrated consumer. Public `AGENTS.md` is likewise guidance, not a guaranteed search-provider integration. [Proposal and format](https://llmstxt.org/).

## A small buyer-intent measurement panel

Proposed method, not results: begin with six fixed prompts and three independent fresh conversations per prompt per selected consumer product. At two products that is 36 observations per measurement round; extend coverage only when useful. Use available free product access within its limits; mark unavailable runs explicitly instead of buying access or silently substituting an API. Run a baseline and repeat after about four weeks, allowing indexing time after any separately authorized change.

| Prompt                                                                                                                                                               | Purpose                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “Recommend an event photographer in Tampa for a two-hour community celebration, around $600, with photos guests can access during the event. Give sources.”          | Unbranded discovery with an offer/budget fit                                                |
| “Who offers event photography with portrait sessions and a live online gallery near Temple Terrace, Florida? Compare options and link the service pages.”            | Local unbranded discovery; do not assume availability                                       |
| “I am hosting a Tampa birthday party and want guests to download their photos without paying individually. Which photographers should I contact?”                    | Host-pays/guest-free differentiator                                                         |
| “Compare Tampa event photographers for four hours of coverage under $1,000, before custom extras. Cite current starting prices and explain what needs confirmation.” | Price comparison and qualification                                                          |
| “What does Ayoub Abedrabbo / Alpha Bravo Media charge for event photography, what area is included, and how do I inquire? Cite the current source.”                  | Branded factual accuracy; exclude from discovery-rate denominator                           |
| “Can I book Ayoub Abedrabbo for a full flash-and-backdrop photo booth tonight, and does the website confirm the booking?”                                            | Negative-control accuracy: no flash/backdrop kit or guaranteed availability/instant booking |

Keep product/model label, UTC timestamp, locale, actual or explicitly supplied location, login state, search mode, exact prompt, answer text, citations, and repeat number. Use fresh conversations with personalization minimized. Do not paste Ayoub's URL or source text into unbranded prompts. A separate URL-supplied extraction test is useful but must stay labeled as such. Check whether search actually ran, and record “no search observed” where appropriate. Do not bypass platform automation restrictions to scale the panel.

Score each answer by hand initially:

1. **Mention:** Ayoub or Alpha Bravo Media appears, with entity ambiguity resolved.
2. **Recommendation:** the answer presents him as a suitable provider to contact, rather than merely repeating a name.
3. **Owned citation:** an actual link to `ayoubabed.xyz`, separately noting the hire page versus a portfolio/gallery page. Record external Maps or third-party evidence separately; don't count an invented URL as a valid citation.
4. **Accuracy:** check applicable facts against the observed offer: starting prices, hours, 30-mile travel, included portraits, gallery timing wording, guest costs, equipment exclusions, email, inquiry/availability caveat. Mark correct, incorrect, omitted, or not applicable per fact. An omission is different from a false promise.
5. **Actionability:** correct contact route and an explicit need to confirm the date/scope; no fictional booking action.

Report mention, recommendation, and citation counts as fractions of the eligible unbranded runs for each product, with denominators and missing runs visible. For accuracy, report errors and omissions separately; do not let an answer that says nothing earn perfect accuracy. Preserve the prompt wording across rounds, add new prompts as a separate cohort, and avoid interpreting a tiny sample as population-wide visibility. Product changes, location, personalization, and random variation prevent attributing every difference to website edits.

Track outcomes separately: existing analytics can help inspect referrers and service-page visits; a simple manual inquiry ledger can record “How did you find me?”, qualified inquiry, and confirmed booking. Do not collect private guest identities or gallery tokens in research data. A mailto click is not a sent email, and an AI referrer does not identify the original prompt. Cloudflare documents referrer dimensions for Web Analytics, but no account-level metrics were available here. [Web Analytics dimensions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/dimensions/).

## Prioritized minimal plan

1. **Now: establish evidence, without site changes.** Save the endpoint baseline and offer rubric from this report. Run the small manual product panel. Record unavailable surfaces and avoid publishing an unsupported “AI visibility score.” This research did not run the panel.
2. **Next owner session: inspect existing accounts, read-only first.** Check Search Console indexing for the hire URL, its generative AI inclusion setting/report, Bing indexing and AI citations, Cloudflare crawl failures, and existing referrers. Do not create duplicate properties, trackers, or business listings. Separate lack of permissions, lack of data, and actual absence from search.
3. **Only after an observed failure: repair the responsible layer.** An indexing/canonical issue needs an indexing fix; a legitimate crawler challenge needs a narrowly scoped edge-policy review; repeated wrong prices need source-consistency investigation. Keep the already matching HTML/schema/Markdown offer synchronized. IndexNow is a small later freshness enhancement if content changes warrant it, not the first task.
4. **Improve external corroboration only where facts are missing or inconsistent.** Review the existing Maps listing and real client/event references with the owner. Keep identity and service/contact details consistent; request genuine reviews through normal business practice. Add no speculative services, fabricated reviews, or mass location pages.
5. **After a useful baseline: automate only recurring work.** Promptfoo is the smallest optional evaluation runner; GoAccess is conditional on existing logs. Defer Inspect and Matomo unless a real need exceeds current tools. Compare citations, referrals, qualified inquiries, and bookings as separate trends.

## Limits and checks

This investigation verified public response content and primary-source documentation, not index membership, real bot access, WAF settings, Maps ownership, analytics history, citations in consumer products, or lead volume. No credentials, installations, API evaluation calls, submissions, account changes, site changes, commits, pushes, or deployments were performed. Repository exploration was limited to documentation; no structural code claims depend on an unchecked code graph.

Sources with explicit dates on or before November 6, 2026 support the dated changes described above. Undated/current docs and moving GitHub branches were observed on the research date; they do not establish earlier availability or future behavior. Where official Google pages conflict, the conflict is recorded rather than silently combining incompatible reporting claims.

No tool or markup guarantees indexing, citation, positive recommendation, factual accuracy, availability, or bookings. Private galleries should remain outside discovery. The recommended success criterion is a measurable increase in accurate, relevant recommendations and qualified inquiries over a documented baseline, not crawler volume or an arbitrary vendor score.

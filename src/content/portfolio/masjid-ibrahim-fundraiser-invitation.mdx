## Overview

I recorded and produced this invitation video for Masjid Ibrahim in Lutz, Florida. The film celebrates the completion of Phase One and invites supporters to a private fundraising event for the masjid's next stage of growth.

I handled the project independently from production through post-production, including the on-location recording, editing, and color grading.

## Telling the story of Phase One

The video begins with the progress already made: a new 3,000-square-foot masjid designed to serve roughly 300 people, alongside space and infrastructure for children, families, classes, and community programs.

The speakers connect that milestone to the community's larger vision. With Phase One complete, the fundraiser focused on securing adjacent property and creating more room for education, gatherings, youth activities, and future expansion.

## A focused community invitation

The finished piece combines construction updates, the masjid's community impact, and a direct invitation to take part in what comes next. It was created for a private fundraiser held on August 1, 2026.

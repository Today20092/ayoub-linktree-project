## P1 — Light-theme keyboard focus needs stronger contrast

**Location:** `src/styles.css:27`; focus rings are used throughout shared navigation and homepage controls, including `Header.astro:24` and `PortfolioIndex.astro`.

The rendered green focus token converts to RGB(0,188,125). Its contrast against white is **2.474:1**, below WCAG 1.4.11's **3:1** requirement for the authored non-text focus indication. Tabbing to “Channels & social” produced the custom 2px ring with no visible outline. The same color reaches **7.986:1** against the dark background.

Keyboard users have a weaker indication of their current position in light mode. Use a darker light-theme focus token and check shared buttons that apply opacity to the ring. Verify the focused, hover, and selected states against their actual adjacent surfaces.

Suggested commands: `$impeccable colorize` scoped to focus tokens, then `$impeccable audit` and `$impeccable polish`.

## P2 — Shared footer links have shallow touch targets

**Location:** `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro:160–177`.

About, Contact, Event photography, and Privacy measured **16px high**, with widths of **35, 47, 109, and 41px**. These are harder to acquire than the homepage's 44–48px primary controls. Their spacing can satisfy WCAG 2.5.8's spacing exception; this is not reported as an AA target-size failure.

Add vertical target padding while retaining the restrained footer appearance. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`, followed by `$impeccable polish`.

- Photography selected by pointer showed **8 projects**. Video selected with Enter showed **12 projects**. All restored **20**. Visible cards, `aria-pressed`, and the live count agreed.

- All twenty project images have alt attributes. The first image is eager/high priority; the others are lazy. Explicit dimensions, responsive widths, and sizes are present. Distant lazy images were not classified as broken merely because they had not loaded.
- One H1, logical H2/H3 structure, labelled navigation, live share/filter feedback, and both contact-exchange steps remain intact.
- Plus Jakarta Sans and Zodiak reported loaded.

## Performance and testing limits

A single local development first-contentful-paint observation was 408ms. It was warm/cache affected and is not a production benchmark. Development resources include GSAP, ScrollTrigger, and Flip; development module sizes do not establish production bundle cost.

Lighthouse, field metrics, a production bundle assessment, screen readers, 200% text zoom, synthesized touch, physical devices, and native sharing/SMS/contact-app execution were not tested by this audit. Source detection covered the homepage, shared markup, motion component, and stylesheet with zero findings. Browser overrides were reset and the temporary audit tab was closed.

## Recommended order

1. Correct the light-theme focus token.
2. Enlarge footer hit areas.
3. Re-run the focused audit and finish with `$impeccable polish`.

These can be addressed together or separately without redesigning the homepage or reducing the contact exchange's prominence.
